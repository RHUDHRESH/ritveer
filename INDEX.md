# ArtisanHub - Complete Business Growth System

## What This Is

A complete, no-fuss business intelligence system that helps artisans (pottery makers, carpenters, weavers, jewelers, etc.) grow their craft businesses by:

1. Understanding who they are and what they do
2. Researching equipment and material suppliers
3. Finding e-commerce sales opportunities
4. Locating local venues for selling/consignment
5. Creating concrete, actionable growth plans

## Get Started in 2 Minutes

### Option 1: Run the Interactive System

```bash
cd artisan_agents
python business_cli.py
```

You'll be asked 12 questions about yourself and your craft. The system then:
- Analyzes your needs
- Researches opportunities
- Generates a complete business plan
- Saves everything to a report

### Option 2: Read the Quick Start

See: `QUICK_START.md`

### Option 3: Understand the Full System

See: `BUSINESS_GROWTH_SYSTEM.md`

## What You Get

When you run the system, you receive:

1. **Equipment Analysis**
   - List of tools/materials you need
   - Where to buy them
   - Estimated costs
   - Supplier contact info

2. **E-Commerce Opportunities**
   - Best marketplaces for your craft (Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, etc.)
   - Fee structures
   - Revenue potential estimates
   - Setup guides and links

3. **Local Selling Venues**
   - Galleries and gift shops for consignment
   - Craft fairs and markets
   - Contact information
   - Commission rates

4. **Growth Plan**
   - 4-phase strategy
   - Week-by-week action items
   - 6-month revenue projections
   - Success metrics

5. **Complete Report**
   - Saved as text file
   - All links included
   - All contacts listed
   - Ready to execute

## How It Works

### 1. You Provide Context
Tell the system about yourself:
- Your name and experience
- What you create
- Where you're located
- Your business goals

### 2. System Analyzes
Based on your craft type:
- Identifies likely equipment needs
- Determines applicable skills
- Suggests appropriate marketplaces
- Recommends suitable venues

### 3. System Researches
Gathers real information about:
- Supplier pricing and locations
- E-commerce platform requirements
- Local business opportunities
- Upcoming events and markets

### 4. System Generates
Creates concrete plans:
- Equipment acquisition strategy
- Online marketplace setup guide
- Local venue scouting plan
- 30-day action plan
- 6-month revenue projections

### 5. You Receive
A text report containing:
- All research findings
- All contact information
- All marketplace links
- Step-by-step action items
- Timeline and milestones
- Success metrics
