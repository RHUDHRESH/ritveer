   - Report generator
   - Full end-to-end workflow

### How It Works

You provide context (who you are, what you do, where you operate):

1. Personal information (name, age, experience)
2. Craft details (type, description, specialties)
3. Location (city, state, country)
4. Business info (current/target income, customers)

System automatically:

1. Analyzes your needs based on craft type
2. Researches equipment, materials, suppliers
3. Finds e-commerce opportunities with revenue estimates
4. Locates galleries, shops, markets, events in your area
5. Generates concrete 4-phase growth plan
6. Creates week-by-week action items
7. Estimates 6-month revenue projections
8. Saves everything to a report

## How to Use

### Start the System

```bash
cd artisan_agents
python business_cli.py
```

### Interactive Session

1. Answer personal questions (name, age, experience)
2. Describe your craft (what you make)
3. Provide location (city, state, country)
4. Share business goals (income, customer targets)
5. Press Enter to let system research
6. Review generated business plan
7. Get comprehensive report saved to disk
